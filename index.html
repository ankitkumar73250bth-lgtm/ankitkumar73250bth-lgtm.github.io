Design And Development of an Arduino Based Omnidirectional Zero-Turn Radius Vehicle with Wireless Control and Quantitative Performance Evaluation

🏛️ 3rd National Conference on Emerging Trends in Science & Technology, 2026
📂 Robotics & Embedded Systems
🔬 Mechanical Engineering
Authors
Ankit Kumar Shishir Snigdh Roy Nitish Kumar Vikash Kumar Md. Waquar Shahid Subodh Kr. Yadav
Abstract

Space constraints in urban and industrial environments demand compact, agile vehicles capable of maneuvring without wide turning arcs. This paper presents the design and theoretical analysis of a proposed 360-degree rotating vehicle with zero-turn radius (ZTR) capability, controlled wirelessly via an Arduino Uno and HC-05 Bluetooth module. The system integrates a BTS7960 high-current motor driver (43A) with four independently driven DC gear motors to achieve omnidirectional movement without positional displacement. Design calculations estimate consistent ZTR performance, command response time below 200 ms, and stable operation across load conditions up to 8 kg.
